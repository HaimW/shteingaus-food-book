Zesty Keto Lemon Bars

1/2 cup butter

1 3/4 cups almond flour

1 cup powdered erythritol א. סוכר

3 lemons

3 eggs s

Melt butter & mix with 1 cup almond flour, 1/4 cup erythritol, and pinch of salt. Press evenly into an 8x8 baking dish lined with parchment paper. Bake for 20 minutes at 350 °F. Cool for 10 minutes.

Zest a lemon into a mixing bowl, juice all the lemons, add the eggs, 3/4 cup erythritol, 3/4 cup almond flour & pinch of salt and combine.

Pour the filling onto the crust & bake for 25 minutes.

Sprinkle with powdered erythritol.
